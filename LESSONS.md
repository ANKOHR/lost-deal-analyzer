# Lessons for KOML

Recorded loss reasons and actual causal explanations are different. Missing
evidence must stay visible as unknown. Monetary opportunity value should never
be called savings or realised revenue.

The deterministic aggregation assumption is tested against independent planted
ground truth. The assumption that this improves real outreach remains untested;
synthetic tests cannot validate adoption, label quality, or commercial demand.

Operating rule: establish provenance and an observation date before aggregation,
and preserve supporting record IDs for every reported reason. Hand-check real
labels during the two-week trial before expanding the tool.
